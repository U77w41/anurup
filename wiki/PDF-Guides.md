# PDF guides

## Turn photos of documents into one PDF

1. Open [JPG to PDF Converter](https://u77w41.github.io/anurup/image-to-pdf/) and add the photos or scans.
2. Use the arrows to put the pages in order.
3. Choose **A4**, **Auto** orientation and **Small file** if the portal has a size limit.
4. Click **Create PDF** and check the preview.

## Compress a PDF to 100 KB, 200 KB or 500 KB

1. Open [Compress PDF to KB](https://u77w41.github.io/anurup/pdf-compressor/).
2. Choose **Target size** and type the limit in KB.
3. For long or colourful scans, turn on **Grayscale**. It makes scans noticeably smaller.
4. Click **Compress PDF** and read the preview before you upload. Target size turns pages into images, so text is no longer selectable. Choose **Lossless** if you need selectable text.

## Merge certificates into one PDF

1. Open [Merge PDF](https://u77w41.github.io/anurup/pdf-merger/) and add the files.
2. Set the order with the arrows. Click the eye icon to check a file.
3. If a file shows **Protected**, click **Unlock** and type its password.
4. Click **Merge**. If the result is over the limit, use **Continue with → Compress PDF**.

## Remove the password from a bank statement or e-Aadhaar

1. Open [Remove PDF Password](https://u77w41.github.io/anurup/pdf-unlock/) and choose the file.
2. Type the password you normally use to open it. For e-Aadhaar this is the first four letters of your name in capitals followed by your year of birth (for example `RAHU1990`).
3. Click **Remove password** and download the unlocked copy.

The text, links and quality stay the same, because ANURUP removes the encryption instead of re-printing the pages. PDFs that only block printing or copying unlock without a password. ANURUP cannot unlock a file if you don't know its password.

## Extract a few pages

1. Open [Split PDF](https://u77w41.github.io/anurup/pdf-splitter/).
2. Click the page thumbnails you want (Shift+click selects a run), or type a range like `1-3, 5`.
3. Click **Extract**.

To split a whole file into single pages or equal parts, pick **Every page separately** or **Every N pages**.

## Fix the page order or a sideways page

Open [Organize PDF Pages](https://u77w41.github.io/anurup/pdf-page-manager/). Drag thumbnails to reorder, use the rotate buttons on a page, and click the bin icon to drop it. Nothing changes in the file until you click **Save**.

## Sign a PDF form and add the date

1. Clean your signature first with the [Signature Cleaner](https://u77w41.github.io/anurup/signature-cleaner/), using a transparent background.
2. Open [Sign & Fill PDF](https://u77w41.github.io/anurup/pdf-sign/) and choose the form.
3. Go to the right page, click **Upload image** (or **From workspace**) and drag the signature into place. Resize it from the corner.
4. Type your name or place in the text box, or click **Add today's date**.
5. Click **Save signed PDF**.

This places an image of your signature on the page. It is not a certificate-based digital signature.

## Add page numbers

Open [Add Page Numbers & Watermark](https://u77w41.github.io/anurup/pdf-stamp/), choose a position and format such as *Page 1 of 9*, and click **Apply to all pages**. Turn on **Don't number the first page** for a cover sheet.
