# FAQ

**Is ANURUP free?**
Yes. There are no accounts, limits or watermarks.

**Does it work on a phone?**
Yes. It works in mobile Chrome and Safari. Very large PDFs may be slow on older phones, because all the work happens on the device.

**Are my files uploaded?**
No. See [Privacy and security](Privacy-and-Security).

**How do I make a photo smaller than 50 KB?**
Use [Resize Image to KB & Pixels](https://u77w41.github.io/anurup/size-targeter/) if the form also gives dimensions, or [Compress Image to KB](https://u77w41.github.io/anurup/image-compressor/) if it only gives a KB limit. Set the maximum to 50.

**The portal says my photo is too small in KB.**
Set **Min size** in Resize Image to KB & Pixels. ANURUP pads the JPG to reach it without changing the picture.

**The portal says the DPI is wrong.**
Resize Image to KB & Pixels and the Passport Size Photo Maker both write the DPI you choose into the JPG.

**What is the difference between JPG and JPEG?**
None. It is the same format with a different file extension.

**Why can't I select text after compressing a PDF?**
Balanced, Strong and Target size turn pages into images to make them much smaller. Use Lossless to keep text selectable.

**Can ANURUP unlock a PDF if I forgot the password?**
No. It needs the password that opens the file.

**Is Sign & Fill PDF a legal digital signature?**
No. It places a picture of your signature on the page. It does not add a certificate-based digital signature.

**Can I use it offline?**
Not yet. You need a connection to load each tool. The processing itself always happens on your device.
