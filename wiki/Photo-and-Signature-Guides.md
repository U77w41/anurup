# Photo and signature guides

Forms usually give two limits for a photo or signature: the **dimensions** (pixels or cm) and the **file size** (KB). Check both on the form before you start.

## Resize a photo to 20-50 KB and 200×230 pixels

1. Open [Resize Image to KB & Pixels](https://u77w41.github.io/anurup/size-targeter/).
2. Upload the photo.
3. Pick the preset *Photo 200×230 px · 20-50 KB*, or type your own width, height, Min size and Max size.
4. Leave *Crop to fill* selected so the face isn't stretched.
5. Click **Resize & compress**. A green tick means the file is within both limits.

If the form gives the size in cm (for example 3.5×4.5 cm at 200 DPI), switch the unit to **cm** and type the DPI. ANURUP converts it to pixels and writes the DPI into the JPG.

## Make a passport size photo (35×45 mm or 2×2 inch)

1. Open [Passport Size Photo Maker](https://u77w41.github.io/anurup/passport-photo/) and upload a front-facing photo.
2. Pick the size. 35×45 mm is used in India, the UK and for Schengen visas, 2×2 inch in the US.
3. Drag the photo and use **Zoom** until the head sits inside the orange oval and the eyes are near the dashed line.
4. Choose a background fill. If the photo has a busy background, run it through [Remove Image Background](https://u77w41.github.io/anurup/background-removal/) first.
5. Click **Generate passport photo**. Turn on the 4×6 inch sheet to print several copies on photo paper at 100% scale.

If the form also has a KB limit, click **Continue with → Resize to size & KB** on the result.

## Prepare a signature for an exam form

1. Sign with a black or blue pen on plain white paper. Take a photo in good light, straight on.
2. Open [Signature Cleaner](https://u77w41.github.io/anurup/signature-cleaner/) and upload it. The result updates as you change settings.
3. Choose **Black** ink and a **White (JPG)** background for most forms. Move the threshold slider if thin strokes vanish.
4. Click **Continue with → Resize to size & KB**, pick the *Signature 140×60 px · 10-20 KB* preset (or type the form's numbers) and resize.

## Change a photo's background to white

1. Open [Remove Image Background](https://u77w41.github.io/anurup/background-removal/) and upload the photo.
2. Click **Remove background**. The first run downloads the AI model, so it takes longer.
3. Pick **White** (or light blue, red, any colour, or transparent). Switching colours afterwards is instant.

## Compress several photos at once

1. Open [Compress Image to KB](https://u77w41.github.io/anurup/image-compressor/) and add all the images.
2. Choose **Target size (KB)**, type the limit and pick **JPG**.
3. Click **Compress**, then **Download all (.zip)**.
